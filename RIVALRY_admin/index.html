PLAY FAIR

Tournament rules.

The basics every player and team agrees to when registering.

  • 01Every player needs their own VALORANT account and must enter the correct in-game ID in the form.
  • 02No cheats, third-party tools, smurfing or account sharing. Violations mean disqualification.
  • 03Be ready at the announced match time. Late teams may lose the match by default.
  • 04Respect everyone. Abuse, hate or harassment is not tolerated, in-game or in chat.
  • 05Match schedules and room details are shared with registered players only.
  • 06The organizers' decision on disputes is final.
QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I register?

Pay the entry fee first, then open the Squad registration form and enter your five-player team details.

How do I pay the entry fee?

Open the Payment page to pay ₹50, then upload your screenshot with your registration. Your entry is confirmed after the organizer verifies the payment.

Can I change my team after registering?

Contact the organizers as early as possible. Changes after the schedule is published may not be possible.

Where will match details be shared?

Schedules, brackets and room details are shared with registered players through the community channel and the contact details you submit.

Is this an official Riot Games event?

No. RIVALRY is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games.
